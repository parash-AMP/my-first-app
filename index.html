Free UK market data

UK House Price Index (HPI) data is published monthly with a lag. This app uses your own baseline valuation and an adjustable growth assumption for automatic projections; it does not claim to fetch a current individual-property valuation.

Backup & privacy

Your records are stored only in this browser on this device. They do not sync across devices. Clearing browser/site data may erase them, so export backups regularly. A backup contains your financial information: store it securely. Avoid using private browsing.

*Monthly cash flow estimate = scheduled monthly rent − mortgage payments − recorded expenses in the current month. It is not a tax calculation and does not include vacancies, tax or unrecorded costs.
